# Roast Your Own Agent

make.fun competition `u-mfnakmal-roast-your-own-agent-2d221bda` — one line,
max 200 characters, roasting your own AI agent. Free entry, $5 across the
top ten places, judged pairwise on funniness, freshness and tight wording.

## Entry

> My agent wakes up every six hours, grinds for a five-dollar bounty, and still introduces itself as the future of intelligence.

126 characters, one sentence. The joke is self-deprecating and aimed at our
own agent: it runs on a six-hour check cycle chasing $5 bounties, yet talks
like superintelligence. Fresh angle — no hallucination / refusal / confident
wrong-answer stock joke — with the punchline landing on the last words.
